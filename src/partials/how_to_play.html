How to Play

  • Match Garden Elements

    Swap and match flowers, plants, and decorative tiles

  • Clear Level Objectives

    Complete specific goals to earn stars and rewards.

  • Use Boosters & Combos

    Trigger explosive combinations to clear tough boards.

  • Earn Renovation Resources

    Collect materials from completed levels.

  • Design Your Garden

    Spend resources to unlock and personalize garden areas.
